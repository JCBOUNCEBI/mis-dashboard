Privacy policy

Salon SBM Dashboard is an internal management dashboard used by Jcbounce / Biguine India and its salon teams. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Why we use it

Only to identify you and to decide which salons' business data you are approved to view. An administrator approves each person individually.

Where it is stored

Your name, email, approval status and last sign-in date are stored in the company's Supabase database. Access to dashboard data is restricted by that approval.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. It is used only inside the company for access control.

Removal

An administrator can revoke your access at any time. To ask for your record to be deleted, contact the administrator below.
